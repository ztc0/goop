Confirmations, three levels of one pattern. The weight of the ask follows how hard the action is to undo, so a heavy dialog always means something real:

LevelExampleShapeEdgeDefault
ReversibleDismiss 3 offersOne-line ask in place (like M2/M6), or no ask and an undo toast--primaryy
CostlyUnfollow 3 companiesDialog with what changes and y/n--accentn keep
IrreversibleDelete all goop data (Privacy M13)Dialog with what goes, what stays, a pink warning and type-to-confirm--erroresc cancel

Tradeoff: the irreversible dialog draws over the dimmed session (inert) with the opencode dialog chrome: panel fill, no radius, bold title left and a muted esc right. It lists counts so you see the size of the loss (412 offers, 38 MB) and what survives (31 GB of models you would not want to download again). Typing the word is slower than y, on purpose: ↵ stays muted until the input reads delete, and esc, the safe choice, is the green default, so a stray enter or a key-repeat can never delete anything. The status line switches to the confirm mode with a pink dot and a brief state label; keys remain in the dialog. Costs: an extra step for a deliberate user, the word is English even when goop talks Spanish (one word to learn, no locale guessing), and three levels means each new action needs a level chosen when it is designed.
